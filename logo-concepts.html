Anniversary mark · concept review

Three directions for a Zion-specific 20-year lockup

Each concept incorporates the location (Zion Lutheran, Fairbanks AK) and the 20-year milestone, and is sized to live alongside — not replace — the registered Celebrate Recovery wordmark. Compare on light, paper, and dark/aurora backgrounds.

v1 · 2026-05-07
Concept 01

Commemorative Seal

A round badge in the tradition of military, civic, and church anniversary marks. Reads as official and commemorative — works as a square favicon, a hero badge, or a foil-stamp on print. Mountain silhouette and aurora pinpricks behind a centered "20" tie it to Alaska.

Best for: favicon, lapel pin, T-shirt back print, Saturday program cover.

Concept 02

Mountain Crest

A taller shield with a cross at the apex, the Alaska Range layered through the body, a banner ribbon for the tagline, and the location seated at the base. Most explicit reference to place and faith of the three; carries the most weight as a heraldic event mark.

Best for: print ad heads, program covers, banners — anywhere you want gravitas.

Concept 03

Aurora Wordmark

Modern, editorial horizontal lockup. Massive "20" anchors the left, an aurora ribbon arcs across, the right column stacks YEARS / Hope & Healing in Alaska / Zion Lutheran · Fairbanks. Cleanest fit for the website header next to the existing CR mark and for email banners.

Best for: site header, email hero, social cover, half-page ad headline.
