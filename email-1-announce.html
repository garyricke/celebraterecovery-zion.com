Dear Friend,

We have exciting news to share: Celebrate Recovery at Zion Lutheran Church in Fairbanks is celebrating 20 years of ministry in Alaska — and we want you to join us for a very special weekend of worship, community, and celebration.

Whether you're facing addiction, grief, anxiety, or any of life's hurts, hang-ups, and habits — you are welcome here. No one should have to face their pain alone.

We're gathering CR groups from across Alaska — Fairbanks, Anchorage, Mat-Su Valley, Juneau, and beyond — for a weekend you won't forget.

  "The Crazy Story of How Celebrate Recovery Came to Alaska" — told by the people who lived it
  Keynote Speaker: Tommy Woodard — comedian, filmmaker, pastor
