This is a short, all-out final-week push — not a slow build. Everything below is front-loaded so ads start working immediately. Facebook needs up to 24 hours to review and approve new ads, so the goal is to get them submitted right away.

The short version

Put the money on Facebook, let it run on Instagram for free at the same time, and skip paid TikTok for this event. Facebook is where our audience already is — the recovery and church community in Alaska skews 35+, we've advertised there before, and it's the best tool for inviting people to a local event. Instagram comes along for the ride through the same Facebook ad setup at no extra effort. TikTok's crowd is younger and a brand-new ad account couldn't get set up, approved, and effective in six days — it's not worth the spend this time.

Which platforms — and how much to lean on each

Why front-loaded? With only six days, there's no time for a slow "build interest over weeks" approach. We spend a little to get approved and warmed up, then concentrate the real money in the final 48 hours when the "this is happening now" message converts best.

Does a bigger budget help?

A fair question — and the honest answer is a little, but not much. For an event like this, money isn't the limit. The size of our audience and Alaska's geography are. Here's the reasoning.

The ceiling, in one line

Our planned ~$400 already covers Fairbanks at a healthy level. Spending much more there doesn't reach new people — it just shows the same people the same ad more often, which stops helping after about 4–5 views.

If we spend…What it actually buysWorth it?
~$400
Fairbanks focus		 Reaches most interested people in Fairbanks & North Pole about 4–5 times — enough to register without becoming repetitive. ✓ Recommended baseline
~$500–600
top-up		 Adds frequency / a little more local reach. Only useful if the first couple of days are clearly converting (registrations coming in, ad still fresh). ~ Only if it's working
$800+
go statewide		 Reaches Anchorage, the Mat-Su Valley, Juneau, Ketchikan — but a Fairbanks event is a flight or a long drive away, so few will attend in person. ✕ Weak for attendance
The geography catch: this is a Fairbanks event. Someone in Anchorage won't fly up for a free Friday night, so dollars spent reaching them rarely turn into people in the room. Statewide spend only makes sense if the goal becomes building awareness of Celebrate Recovery across Alaska rather than filling these two days.

What fills seats better than more ad money

For a local faith event, the strongest tools are mostly free:

  1. 1
    The email list (already built in Email Octopus) — warm, trusted people, at almost no cost.
  2. 2
    CR Alaska leaders & Fairbanks churches sharing the post — a share from a CR leader or pastor reaches this crowd better than any paid ad.
  3. 3
    Leading with the free Friday night — the lowest-barrier invitation we have.
  4. 4
    Boosting one post to our existing page followers — cheap reach to people who already know us.
Bottom line: start at ~$400 focused on Fairbanks, watch the first two days of registrations, and add $100–200 only if it's clearly converting. Above roughly $600 we're paying for repetition, not new people — at that point the free options above do more good than extra ad spend.

How to set it up (plain steps)

In Facebook Ads Manager, create one campaign with the goal set to Traffic (sends people to the website) or Engagement. Then:

  1. 1
    Who sees it (location): Start with Fairbanks + North Pole (a 25-mile radius). If budget allows, add Anchorage, the Mat-Su Valley (Wasilla/Palmer), Juneau, and Ketchikan.
  2. 2
    Who sees it (people): Ages 30–65+, all genders. Add interests like Celebrate Recovery, Christianity, recovery/sobriety, church, Christian music. Keep it fairly broad — Alaska is a small market.
  3. 3
    Placements: Choose Advantage+ / Automatic placements so Facebook and Instagram (feed, Stories, Reels) are all on. Upload all three image sizes so each spot looks right.
  4. 4
    Schedule: Set the end date to end of day June 6 so nothing runs (or charges) after the event.
  5. 5
    Link: Send everyone to celebraterecovery-zion.com with the “Learn More” button.
  6. 6
    Before you publish: get Robert's approval on the artwork and wording (promotional materials need sign-off before they go live).
One tip: the easiest path, if Ads Manager feels like a lot, is to make a normal post on the Celebrate Recovery / Zion Facebook page using Angle A's wording and the Square image, then hit Boost Post and apply the location/age settings above. It's the same engine with fewer buttons.
