Identity & Logo Usage

Approved Backgrounds
Important: The primary logo file is white. It must always appear on a dark background — navy, dark navy, CR orange, or black. Never place it on white, off-white, or light gray backgrounds without acquiring the dark-color version from CR National.
Clear Space
1× logo height clearance 1× logo height

Maintain a minimum clear space equal to the height of the logo emblem on all four sides.

Do / Don't
Do
  • Use on navy, dark navy, or orange backgrounds
  • Scale proportionally — never stretch or squish
  • Maintain minimum width of 120px (digital) / 1.5 in (print)
  • Use SVG format for all digital applications
  • Request high-res EPS/PDF from CR National for large-format print
Don't
  • Place on light, white, or patterned backgrounds
  • Recolor, outline, or add drop shadows to the logo
  • Crop, rotate, or alter the proportions
  • Use at sizes below 120px wide (digital) or 1" wide (print)
  • Place on low-contrast or busy photographic backgrounds

Imagery Guidelines

Use Images That Are…

  • Authentic and unposed — real moments of connection
  • Community-focused — groups, pairs, people together
  • Warm and hopeful — good light, positive expressions
  • Inclusive of diverse ages and backgrounds
  • Outdoors or natural Alaska settings when relevant
  • High-contrast, print-safe (300 DPI minimum for print)

Avoid Images That Show…

  • Clinical or sterile environments suggesting treatment
  • Distressed, stigmatizing, or crisis-focused subjects
  • Isolated individuals looking hopeless or defeated
  • Generic stock photo aesthetics (too clean, too posed)
  • Imagery suggesting only addiction (70% of attendees are not)
  • Low-resolution images (below 72 DPI for web, 300 for print)
Speaker Photo — Tommy Woodard
Photo Usage Notes
  • Used for speaker bio sections on all media
  • Pair with navy background for contrast and warmth
  • Crop from shoulders up for email and social use
  • Do not alter skin tone, add filters, or distort
  • Usage subject to contract approval via Liz Gerdes

Print File Checklist

Before Submitting to the Printer

  • Export as PDF/X-1a (CMYK, embedded fonts)
  • All colors converted from RGB to CMYK
  • All fonts embedded (Montserrat + Open Sans)
  • 300 DPI minimum for all raster images
  • 0.125" bleed added on all sides

  • Logo saved from SVG source, not rasterized screenshot

Voice & Tone

Do Write…

  • Warm and welcoming — lead with belonging
  • Hopeful and forward-looking, not crisis-focused
  • Community-first language ("we," "together," "join us")
  • Faith-centered without heavy doctrine
  • Specific and concrete (real dates, real people, real place)
  • Inclusive — CR is for all hurts, not just addiction
  • Action-oriented CTAs ("Register," "Join Us," "Come")

Avoid…

  • Clinical or treatment language ("patients," "therapy")
  • Shame-based framing ("if you're struggling with...")
  • Assuming only addicts attend (only ~30% are)
  • Heavy doctrinal language that excludes seekers
  • Vague platitudes ("life-changing experience")
  • Urgency that feels manipulative or fear-based
  • Jargon non-CR people won't understand
